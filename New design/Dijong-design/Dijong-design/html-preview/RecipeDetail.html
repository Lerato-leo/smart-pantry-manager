Chicken curry with rice

Ingredients

Chicken thighs 1 kg
Rice 2 cups
Onion 1
Cream 125 ml
Curry powder Missing 2 tbsp

Method

  1. 1Brown the chicken thighs in a hot pot, then set aside.
  2. 2Soften the chopped onion in the same pot for 5 minutes.
  3. 3Stir in the curry powder and cook for 1 minute.
  4. 4Return the chicken, add the cream and simmer for 25 minutes.
  5. 5Cook the rice while the curry simmers and serve together.
